Artisanship from Hazara

Born in the Misty Pines of Abbottabad

ALHA was established with a singular vision: to bridge the timeless opulence of royal Eastern perfumery with the refined subtlety of Grasse craftsmanship, rooted in the serene mountain climate of Abbottabad.

Operating as an exclusive, online-only fragrance house allows us to direct every resource into sourcing the finest raw oils—Cambodian agarwood, Isparta roses, and wild mountain mosses—without the bloated markups of commercial retail storefronts.
